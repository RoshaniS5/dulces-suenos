I saw the glint of the knife cutting through the air.

I nearly yelped, but I had learned to keep my mouth shut all this time.

“Here we go, this’ll fix you right up.” I turned my face away, but she caught it within her tight grip. “Don’t move, mija.”

Despite her stern tone, I kept trying to pull my face away. Twisting my neck in vain.

I heard my neck bones crack as she yanked my face in one swift motion.

I met her eyes—a deep cacao brown like mine. Yet, they had no hint of warmth in them. Her gaze was icy cold. Her smile remained, however, as if it was a sticker on her face that she wore consistently.

“I said, ‘Don’t. Move.’”

At that moment, it was as if a cold, bony finger ran down the back of my head and along my spine before nipping at my heels.

I was frozen from how sadistic she seemed.

Practically inhuman.

I soon felt a burning sensation erupt at the corners of my mouth and it wasn’t long before I could taste the blood pooling on and around my teeth. A few tears escaped from my eyes, warm at the rims.

I felt her thumb run across my tear-stained cheeks.

“There you go,” she whispered. “Sensitive, just like a girl.”

The knife clattered on the dining table, blood splattered all over its blade.
